## Quality bar check

1. Would a viewer who's never seen us understand the hook in 1 second? ✅ "Sara invested for 10 years. Kevin invested for 30. Sara won." — clear in first 5 seconds.
2. Does the cover match the video's promise — no bait? ✅ Cover shows the comparison outcome; video delivers the same argument.
3. Is there ONE specific number / year / person / demo in the spoken part? ✅ $281,000 vs $244,000, spoken out loud.
4. Is the closing a STATEMENT (not a passive question)? ✅ "Time does the heavy lifting. Not you."
5. Is the CTA one clear action? ✅ "Follow for more boring math that actually works."
