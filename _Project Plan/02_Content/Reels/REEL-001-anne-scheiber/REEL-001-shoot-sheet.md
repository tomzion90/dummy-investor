# 🎙️ MAIN SCRIPT

She wasn't a banker.

She was a tax auditor at the IRS —

retired in 1944

with five thousand dollars in savings

and decades of notes

on which companies survived.

She bought blue-chip stocks.

Coca-Cola.

Schering-Plough.

Pepsi.

And then she didn't sell —

through the fifties,

sixties,

seventies,

eighties.

Fifty-one years.

When she died in 1995,

the account was worth

twenty-two million dollars.

That's about eighteen percent a year —

for half a century —

without picking a single moonshot.

---

# 🎙️ ENDING (FOLLOW CTA — brand standard)

What did the work

was time,

not timing.

Follow for more boring math

that actually works.
