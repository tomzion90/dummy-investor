# 🎙️ SCRIPT

## 🎙️ HOOK

This investor made one decision in 30 years.

Same amount.

Same day.

Every month.

That's it.

---

## 🎙️ MAIN SCRIPT

It's called dollar-cost averaging.

You pick a number —

say, two hundred dollars —

and you invest it on the first of every month.

Market up?

You buy.

Market down?

You buy more shares for the same price.

No news.

No guessing.

No timing.

Over 30 years,

two hundred dollars a month

at the S&P 500's historical average

becomes four hundred and fifty-two thousand dollars.

You put in seventy-two thousand.

---

## 🎙️ ENDING — FOLLOW CTA (brand standard)

DCA doesn't need timing,

luck,

or a finance degree.

It needs a calendar

and a standing order.

Follow for more boring math that actually works.
